**A practical GCIIP prompt to build a Financial Budget app**

**Challenge**

Help me structure my challenge using GCIIP (Goal, Context, Intent, Instruction, Presentation) focused on building an app to track by financial Budget, illustrating the existing expenses incurred for the month and balance after deducting all the fixed and variable expenses.

**GCIIP Challenge: Great Financial Budgeting tool**

**Goal**

Help me identify and track my monthly expenses vs Budget allocated for the month.

My priorities are to:

1. Generate an overview of my spent (actual and forecast) in a visual table format to see how much balance left or savings per month from the Budget allocated.  
2. Create an app that is useful and easy to capture expenses by just snapshot (receipts or credit card statement snapshots) or manual input by Users.  
3. Analyse and propose what is the best budget for future months based on category of spent previously.  
4. Help me rank these priorities and convert them into visual charts.

**Context**

I have retired and want to track and plan my financial better by transparency of my spent in the following categories:

* **Home Loan repayments**  
* **Fixed monthly utilities:**  
  * Unifi Home broadband  
  * Mobile phone lines  
  * 2nd home utilities and maintenance fees  
* **Meals in restaurant**  
* **Online shopping**  
* **Medical expenses** (includes doctor’s and pharmacies costs)  
* **Grocery shopping**  
* **Car expenses** (petrol, toll, maintenance)  
* **Ad-Hoc expenses**

Budget allocation per month: MYR6000 per month.

I prefer the monthly spent are below my allocated budget each month.

 

**Intent**

Analyse my spent situation and help me:

* Clarify my top three spent (apart from the home loan repayment & utilities).  
* Identify category (apart from the loan repayment and monthly utilities costs) that could further reduced on monthly basis.  
* Suggest three to five suitable budget ideas that can optimise my monthly budget without losing the existing lifestyle comfort.  
* Compare them by demand, enjoyment, flexibility, and risk.  
* Recommend one primary idea and one backup idea.  
* Design a practical 2-month (or 8-week) plan for a better budget planning.  
* Highlight common pitfalls, unrealistic expectations, and activities I should avoid.  
   

**Instruction**

Act as a practical financial planner who understands good budgeting, tracking of expenses, and the needs of retirees.

Ask only the most important questions. Then:

1. Translate my inputs into specific targets.  
2. Recommend an AI tool that can assist in monitoring my expenses and track them against my Budget to save time and improve budgeting quality.  
3. Include weekly deliverables to show:

   Ø  Weekly spent vs budget on the non-fixed expenses (apart from the home loan repayment & utilities) by category,

   Ø  Overall balance left for the month to spend

   Ø  Propose a simple recommendation based on in-depth analysis done if the expenses exceed the budget allocation for the month.

    

**Presentation**

Present the answer in this format:

1. My top three spent in Pie Charts (each for the Week and Month) based on all category.  
2. Top 3 expenses (excluding the Loan repayment and utilities) per week– in table format (with value in MYR$).  
3. Overview on Comparison Actual Spent vs Monthly Budget Allocation (Total) in a table format. Residual to spend in positive value, while deficit shown in negative value.  
4. Recommendation for better savings in simple bullet points and concise explanation on what is the best budget for future months based on category of spent previously.  
5. Risks and adjustments  
6. Next three actions to take this week
